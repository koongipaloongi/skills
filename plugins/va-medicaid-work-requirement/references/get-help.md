# Where to get free help

Use **only** the contact details below. Never make up or guess a phone number, address or website.

## Cover Virginia (Virginia Medicaid call center)
- Phone: **1-855-242-8282**
- TTY: **1-888-221-1590**
- Live chat and website: **https://coverva.dmas.virginia.gov**
- Report a new address, phone number or email: **https://commonhelp.virginia.gov** or call Cover Virginia
- Best for: checking whether you're in Medicaid Expansion, asking about exemptions, what to send, and updating contact information
- Sources: [FAQ]; [COVERVA]; [BULLETIN]

## Your local department of social services
- Find your office: **https://www.dss.virginia.gov/localagency/**
- Best for: questions about your case, renewals and documents
- Source: linked from [DMAS]

## Free legal aid
- Statewide legal aid line: **866-LEGLAID (866-534-5243)**. This connects you to your local legal aid office.
- Website: **https://www.virginialawhelp.org**, maintained by the Virginia Poverty Law Center
- Best for: denials, lost coverage, appeals, or anything unclear
- Source: https://www.virginialawhelp.org/en/get-legal-help (checked 2026-09-26)

## Appeals

- You can appeal a denial or loss of coverage under the work requirement. Follow the appeal instructions in the **Notice of Adverse Action**. [FAQ]
- **The deadline printed on your notice is the one that counts.** Always say this first.
- General appeal rules, from the DMAS *Client Appeals FAQ* (dated **May 2021**, so older than the work requirement):
  - "In most cases, the time limit is 30 days from the date you receive the written notice of action." The notice is "presumed" to arrive "five days after the agency mails the notice."
  - **Keeping coverage during an appeal:** you "may request to have your coverage continued during the appeal process if you file your appeal request before the date coverage is terminated or within 10 days of the date stated on the notice of action." "Not all cases qualify." If you lose the appeal, "you may have to pay back the costs of medical care received during the period of continued coverage."
- **How to file an appeal with DMAS** [DMAS appeals page; Cover Virginia appeals page]:
  - Online: https://www.dmas.virginia.gov/appeals/ (the AIMS portal)
  - Email: appeals@dmas.virginia.gov
  - Phone: 804-371-8488 (TTY: 1-800-828-1120)
  - Fax: 804-452-5454
  - Mail or in person: Appeals Division, Department of Medical Assistance Services, 600 E. Broad Street, Richmond, VA 23219
  - You can use the DMAS form or write your own letter. "Include a full copy of your notice of action and any documents you want DMAS to review."
- **Coverage ended because renewal paperwork wasn't returned:** "you have 90 days after you lost coverage to return the renewal paperwork." [DMAS appeals page]
