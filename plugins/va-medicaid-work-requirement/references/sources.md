## Known differences between sources

- **The September 2026 FAQ replaced the July 2026 FAQ.** It changed these points:
  - start date for current members ("scheduled renewal date of March 31, 2027 or later")
  - credit-hour conversion (the ×3 × 4.33 formula was removed; there's now a 4-credits → 16-hours example and "6 credit hours … per week" ≈ half-time)
  - no timing rule for exclusions
  - SSI and SSDI recipients are exempt
  - E7 and E8 no longer say the applicant "must request"; how to request "will be provided at a later date"
  - 2027 self-attestation, with no doctor's note needed
  - a Notice of Non-Compliance with a 30-day deadline
  - the Correspondence Center mailing address
- DMAS's *Resources for Work, Education & Volunteer* page and Cover Virginia still link to older FAQs (July 2026, and Jan 28, 2026 on Cover Virginia). The September 2026 FAQ is the newest and is the one used here.

- The TANF/SNAP work requirement exclusion (X4) and the SUD treatment program exclusion (X7) appear **only in the FAQ**.
- For tribal members, the DMAS page says "Members of federally recognized tribes," while the FAQ says "American Indian or Alaska Native."
- The Cover Virginia page links to an older DMAS URL (`/news-updates/work-community-engagement-requirements/`).
- The appeal deadlines come from a 2021 FAQ that was written before the work requirement.
