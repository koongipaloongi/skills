# Exemptions: exclusions and exceptions

Source codes ([FAQ], [DMAS], [BULLETIN]) are defined in [rules.md](rules.md) and listed in [sources.md](sources.md). [FAQ] is the **September 2026** FAQ, and "Q" numbers refer to its questions.

"Individuals do not need to meet the work requirement if they are exempt. An individual is exempt if they have an 'exclusion' or an 'exception'." [FAQ Q6]

## A. First, is the person in Medicaid Expansion at all?

The requirement doesn't apply to people in a different type of Medicaid. [FAQ Q2] [COVERVA]

- Age **18 or younger**, or **65 or older**. [DMAS]
- In pregnancy or postpartum coverage, children's coverage, or coverage for adults with a disability. [FAQ Q2]
- Eligible for Medicare. Expansion doesn't cover people eligible for Medicare. [FAQ Q2] The DMAS page lists "People who qualify for Medicare Part A or are enrolled in Medicare Part A or Part B." [DMAS]

If someone doesn't know which type of Medicaid they have, send them to Cover Virginia (see [get-help.md](get-help.md)).

### When exclusions count
- The September FAQ lists **no timing rule** for exclusions. (The July FAQ's "at least one day in the month…" rule was removed.) An exclusion that applies **now**, when the person applies or renews, clearly counts.
- An exclusion that applied **earlier** also counts as exception **E3** (below) if it applied in the month before applying (for applicants), or at any time since the last renewal (for members).

## C. Exceptions [FAQ Q6]

"To count, the exception must have happened either (for applicants) the month before their Medicaid application or (for current members) anytime since their last renewal."

| ID | Exception (Sept 2026 FAQ wording) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | "Being under age 19" | |
| E2 | "Being enrolled in certain other types of Medicaid coverage (children's coverage, pregnancy coverage, coverage for Supplemental Security Income recipients)" | |
| E3 | "Any of the exclusions described above" | So an exclusion (X1–X10) that applied during this timing window counts, even if it doesn't apply now. |
| E4 | "Incarcerated in the past 3 months" | |

Say this may need to be requested, that DMAS hasn't said how yet, and to report it on the application or renewal and ask Cover Virginia. |
| E8 | "Traveled to get medical care for themselves or a dependent. The care must be for a serious or complex medical condition, and care cannot be available in their community" | Same note as E7. |

## D. How the state checks
- "Whenever possible, Virginia Medicaid will use information already available to the state … including determining whether someone has met the work requirement or is exempt." [FAQ Q5]
- "In 2027, in most cases, the information you put on your application or renewal will be all you need to show you qualify for an exemption … Additional requirements will apply in 2028." [FAQ Q5]
- Medical or disability exemption: "No. For 2027, you are not required to provide any documentation of your medical condition or disability … Additional documentation requirements will apply in 2028." [FAQ Q8]
