# Exemptions: exclusions and exceptions

Source codes ([FAQ], [DMAS], [BULLETIN]) are defined in [rules.md](rules.md) and listed in [sources.md](sources.md).

The FAQ splits exemptions into **exclusions** and **exceptions**. They differ in **which month counts**.

## A. First, is the person in Medicaid Expansion at all?

The requirement doesn't apply to people who are in a different type of Medicaid. [FAQ] [COVERVA]

- Age **18 or younger**, or **65 or older**. [DMAS] ("Children who are 18 or younger and adults who are 65 or older")
- In pregnancy or postpartum coverage, children's coverage, or coverage for adults with a disability. [FAQ]
- Eligible for Medicare. Expansion doesn't cover people eligible for Medicare. [FAQ] The DMAS page lists "People who qualify for Medicare Part A or are enrolled in Medicare Part A or Part B." [DMAS]

If someone doesn't know which type of Medicaid they have, send them to Cover Virginia (see [get-help.md](get-help.md)).

## B. Exclusions

**Timing:** "The exclusion must be met for at least one day in the month that they apply or, if a current member, in the month that their renewal is processed." [FAQ]

|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | "Pregnancy, or being within 12 months of the end of a pregnancy" | [DMAS] [BULLETIN] | |
| X2 | "Being a former foster care youth who is 25 or younger" | [DMAS] [BULLETIN] | [DMAS] words it as "People who left foster care at age 18 and are currently 25 or younger." |
| X3 | "Being a parent, guardian, caretaker relative, or family caregiver of a dependent child age 13 or younger, or of a person of any age who has a disability (as defined in the Americans with Disabilities Act)" | [DMAS] [BULLETIN] | [BULLETIN] says "children under 14," which means the same thing. | X8 | "Incarceration" | [DMAS] [BULLETIN] | |

## C. Exceptions

**Timing:** a new applicant qualifies if an exception applies "in the month before the month they apply." A current member qualifies if an exception applies "during any one month since their last renewal." [FAQ]

| ID | Exception (FAQ wording) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | "Being under age 19" | |
| E2 | "Being enrolled in certain other types of Medicaid coverage (children's coverage, pregnancy coverage, coverage for SSI recipients)" | |
| E3 | "Meeting a specified exclusion (during the review period, but not in the month that the application is submitted or renewal is processed)" | So an exclusion from Section B that applied **earlier** in the review period still counts, as an exception. |
| E4 | "Having been incarcerated in the past 3 months" | [DMAS]: "released from incarceration in last 3 months" |
| E5 | "Living in a locality with an unemployment rate that is at least 8% or is 1.5x the national unemployment rate" | The plugin **doesn't know** which localities qualify. Send to help. |
| E6 | "Living in a locality under federal emergency or disaster declaration" | The plugin doesn't know current declarations. Send to help. |
| E7 | "Receiving inpatient hospital care, or nursing facility care, or institutional care (including psychiatric), or care of similar acuity level" | ⚠️ "this exception must be requested by the applicant." Tell the person they have to ask for it. |
| E8 | "Having to travel to get medical care, for self or a dependent, for a serious or complex medical condition, because that care is not available in the community" | ⚠️ "this exception must be requested by the applicant." Tell the person they have to ask for it. |

## D. How the state checks

"Virginia Medicaid will first use information from the member's application, case record and other information available to the state to see if a Medicaid Expansion member meets an exclusion or exception. Starting in 2028, the state may request additional documentation to confirm that the member or applicant meets certain exclusions or exceptions." [FAQ]

## E. Not covered by these sources: send to help

- Whether a specific health condition counts as "medically frail" (X9)
- Which localities currently qualify under E5 or E6
- Custody or caregiving arrangements that don't clearly fit X3
- Whether a specific program counts as a "SUD treatment program" (X7)
